Origin & Ethos

Code is an instrument of conviction.

I'm Parth Mohindroo, an undergraduate Computer Science student at Newton School of Technology in India.

What began as tinkering with scripts rapidly evolved into a rigorous discipline: writing deterministic software, mastering the terminal, and peeling back the abstraction layers between high-level code and the kernel.

I firmly embrace the UNIX philosophy — write programs that do one thing cleanly, communicate with clear interfaces, and stay resilient under failure. When I'm not writing Python or tuning Linux dotfiles, I'm analyzing algorithmic bounds and dissecting distributed patterns.
